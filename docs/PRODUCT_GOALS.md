## Goal card

- **Target/job:** players drawn to consequential narrative worlds who want their choices remembered visibly without confusing controls or forced cloud conversation.
- **Pain hypotheses:** portals are attractive but objectives unclear; choices feel cosmetic; NPC memory invents facts; currency feedback is ambiguous; visual/control friction interrupts immersion.
- **Outcome:** an unassisted player completes the established time-travel causal chain, explains a consequence, and restores progress.
- **Unknown-baseline measures:** activation = complete the first meaningful trade; retention = voluntarily replay a different choice; value = correctly explain a cross-era consequence; trust = distinguish authored/local/Meta modes and save boundaries; quality = state isolation, no duplicate rewards, readable labels, and actual-device control/comfort evidence.
- **Proposed pilot:** five approved adult players; four finish the finite demo unaided and explain one causal consequence; all distinguish dialogue modes; no observed unrecoverable save loss. Any discomfort permits an immediate stop and is never counted as participant failure. Headset tests require actual hardware.
- **Paid hypothesis:** later polished authored story expansions or a complete-game purchase only after value and rights validation. No currency pressure, paid calls, payments, public preview, or submission are authorized by this plan.

These goals are hypotheses, not customer findings, proven demand, health outcomes, or promises about revenue, bestseller status, or competition results. No telemetry is authorized to obtain the missing metrics.

## Execution addendum

Continue only in ELSEWHERE's original task and established working branch. Do not replace the task or convert a promising prototype into an infinite feature list. Pin current code, recorded requirements, and actual worker state. The immediate outcome is a comprehensible, consequential local demo: guest entry, future-self encounter, nineteenth-century decision, grounded later recall, distinct future consequence, and reliable encrypted reload. A judge-ready demonstration and a commercially valuable game are separate hypotheses; neither is guaranteed by passing tests.

Test five pain hypotheses against the actual build: the next action is hard to discover; choices appear cosmetic; remembered dialogue invents facts; time costs or rewards are unclear; overlapping signs or awkward controls interrupt play. Prefer a complete causal experience over adding another world. Make state consequences and trade costs legible without explaining every mystery. NPCs may recall recorded fictional decisions, not invent personal knowledge or rewrite authoritative state. Preserve authored local dialogue as the default and distinguish any separately authorized model mode.

Use R5 to frame autonomy and competence as testable design goals. Give the player understandable choices and enough feedback to attribute a consequence to an action. Compare the present sign/control flow with a candidate repair in the same scene; ask players to predict and explain outcomes. Reject a change if it improves button discovery but removes meaningful choice, or if consequences remain unintelligible. This is not a claim about dopamine, therapy, or guaranteed retention. D2 informs comfort preferences and locomotion review, but desktop software rendering cannot establish Quest comfort or real-device performance.

Prepare five consenting adult play sessions, pending approval to recruit, observe, or record. Use the finite demo without paid APIs or public deployment. Observe unassisted navigation, the first time trade, a cross-era consequence, and save restoration. Ask players to explain what changed, why it changed, and whether dialogue was authored, local-model, or remote. Proposed formative criteria are four of five completing the sequence and explaining one consequence unaided, all five understanding runtime disclosure, and no observed unrecoverable save loss. Let anyone stop immediately for discomfort; do not pressure continued play or relabel discomfort as user error. These gates do not prove market demand or headset acceptance.

Measure activation as a meaningful completed trade, not launch; retention as voluntary alternative-choice replay, not time trapped in a session; value as consequence comprehension; trust as runtime/save comprehension; quality as state and input correctness. All baselines remain unknown until observed. Sources are separately consented observer task sheets and optional follow-up, not hidden analytics. Show numerator and denominator and nonresponse/withdrawal separately. Tiny pilots are qualitative diagnostics, not statistically supported prevalence, efficacy, or demand. No collection is activated. Keep private data out of research materials and the public repository.

A later paid-value hypothesis can examine substantial authored expansions or a complete-game offer, but this authorizes no purchase, checkout, or monetization of essential controls. Verify current competition requirements from the actual official program source before making a submission claim. D2 is not an eligibility document. Live Meta capability, any paid call, public HTTPS preview, real video publication, and submission retain their individual gates. Existing mocks and local alternatives are not substitutes for eligible live evidence.

Close concrete findings through implementation, same-scene regression checks, and independent rendered review. Report actual hardware and software, unresolved comfort/device gates, and verified working-branch evidence. Never report reduced draw calls as a measured speedup without equivalent-condition timing. Preserve a clear checkpoint until product acceptance and separately authorized release/submission are established.

## Evidence register

These references were supplied with the goals addendum. They inform hypotheses and measures; they are not ELSEWHERE customer research. They do not authorize participant studies or establish product efficacy, demand, or commercial outcomes.

- **R1, Rodden, Hutchinson and Fu, CHI 2010:** [HEART and goals-to-metrics](https://research.google/pubs/measuring-the-user-experience-on-a-large-scale-user-centered-metrics-for-web-applications/). A measurement framework, not evidence that this product has demand. Use outcome signals instead of feature counts; it does not require remote tracking.
- **R2, Gollwitzer and Brandstätter, 1997:** [Implementation Intentions and Effective Goal Pursuit](https://sparq.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj19021/files/media/file/gollwitzer_brandstatter_1997_-_implementation_intentions_effective_goal_pursuit.pdf). Includes a correlational first study and experimental subsequent studies. Supports testing specific cue-action planning, not claiming all reflection improves health or guarantees action.
- **R3, Epstein et al., UbiComp 2015:** [A Lived Informatics Model of Personal Informatics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12435389/). Descriptive research on everyday tracking, including lapsing and resuming; motivates testing nonjudgmental return rather than assuming uninterrupted streaks are necessary. Not a clinical intervention trial.
- **R4, Karpicke and Blunt, Science 2011:** [Retrieval practice and meaningful learning](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21252317/) and [paper](https://ctl.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/KarpickeBlunt2011.pdf). Experiments concern learning science texts, not every philosophical or reflective activity. Supports testing recall and inference prompts for factual material, not scoring personal values.
- **R5, Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski, 2006:** [The Motivational Pull of Video Games](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf). Four studies investigate motivation through self-determination theory. Use autonomy and competence as design hypotheses, not proof of retention or a dopamine claim.
- **R6, Green and Brock, November 2000:** [Narrative transportation study, author institution record](https://researchconnect.buffalo.edu/en/publications/the-role-of-transportation-in-the-persuasiveness-of-public-narrat/). Studies narrative involvement and persuasion. It is not a recipe for sales, literary quality, or moral correctness. Only the accessible institutional record was consulted in the supplied research.
- **D1, Day One official documentation, accessed 2026-10-28, page date unspecified:** [privacy/security FAQ](https://dayoneapp.com/privacy-faqs/) and [exporting entries](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/settings/exporting-entries/). Observable comparator: export options are described independently of subscription status. This establishes a competing product capability, not verified unmet demand or equivalent security architecture.
- **D2, Meta official documentation, supplied as checked 2026-10-28:** [locomotion preferences](https://developers.meta.com/vr/design/locomotion-user-preferences/) and [best practices](https://developers.meta.com/vr/design/locomotion-best-practices/). Supports explicit comfort choices and control design. It does not verify a particular competition's current eligibility or judge rubric. Meta event/rules pages were blocked by the managed proxy in the latest research check; recheck current official rules and documentation before release claims.
