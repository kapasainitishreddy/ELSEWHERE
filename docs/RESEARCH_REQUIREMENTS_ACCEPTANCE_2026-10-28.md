## Interaction decision

Research directly supports honoring WebXR's session input and animation lifecycle; it does not prescribe this game's signage layout. The screenshot's redundant desktop overlays had a concrete source: controller choice/comfort panels were constructed and raycast in desktop mode. They are now session-gated and absent from desktop picking. Remaining world signage uses a 14-unit decorative radius, a 9-unit action radius, and a cap of three visible labels. Action availability remains via the separate nearby `E` cue and existing game/UI paths. The distance/cap rule is a testable layout hypothesis based on the capture, not a claim about universal player preference.

No comparable-game or user-study claim was used to justify this change. Audience, demand, accessibility on physical devices, and any commercial impact remain hypotheses until tested with people and hardware.
