## Proposed short demo beats and the evidence each would show

These are product-evidence beats, not a verified map to the current official rubric.

1. **Start and goal (Impact, Demo):** choose the instant unsaved guest path; explain that this is a fictional possible future, not a prediction.
2. **Play the short journey (Impact, Demo):** meet the imagined older self, travel to 1889, build an invention and choose gift, patent or auction.
3. **Show the deterministic consequence (Impact, Technical, Demo):** return to 2087 and point out the changed city, NPC memory and grounded causal receipt. Keep invention and ownership facts sourced from game state.
4. **Explain the model boundary (Meta, Technical):** show opt-in and authored offline behavior honestly. Replace mock/authored labeling with a real Meta example only after published rules confirm the integration and the separately approved live synthetic probe and consent flow are proven.
5. **Close with reproducibility (Technical, Demo):** show validated tests and code boundaries, then a working judge-accessible preview and recording once authorized and verified.

The first four beats are a local demo outline. They do not satisfy public access, live Meta, video, entrant eligibility, or final submission gates by themselves.
